# Post-Quantum Readiness Toolkit

**A practical approach to cryptographic risk assessment and post-quantum migration planning.**

## Overview

The transition to post-quantum cryptography is becoming an important consideration for organizations that depend on digital trust, long-term data confidentiality, and secure communications.

Preparing for this transition requires more than identifying cryptographic algorithms. Organizations need visibility into where cryptography is used, an understanding of business and technology dependencies, and a clear process for prioritizing migration activities.

The Post-Quantum Readiness Toolkit provides a structured approach to assessing cryptographic exposure, evaluating organizational readiness, and developing a risk-based migration plan.

## Objectives

- Establish visibility into cryptographic assets and dependencies.
- Identify systems that require assessment for post-quantum migration.
- Prioritize risks based on business impact, data sensitivity, and technical dependencies.
- Support migration planning, governance, and executive oversight.
- Track readiness through defined responsibilities and measurable progress indicators.

## Core Components

The toolkit is being developed around five practical capabilities:

1. **Cryptographic Inventory:** A structured template for identifying cryptographic assets, dependencies, ownership, and migration considerations.
2. **PQC Readiness Assessment:** A questionnaire to evaluate organizational preparedness across governance, discovery, risk, technology, and planning.
3. **Risk Prioritization:** An initial screening matrix to help prioritize cryptographic assets and dependencies for further assessment.
4. **Migration Roadmap:** A phased approach to governance, discovery, assessment, architecture, testing, implementation, and continuous improvement.
5. **Executive Reporting:** Suggested indicators to communicate readiness, dependencies, risks, and progress to leadership.

## Intended Audience

This toolkit is intended for CISOs, security architects, enterprise architects, technology risk professionals, cryptography teams, and organizations preparing for post-quantum migration.

## Methodology

The toolkit follows five stages:

1. **Discover:** Identify cryptographic assets and dependencies.
2. **Assess:** Evaluate exposure, data sensitivity, business impact, and readiness.
3. **Prioritize:** Determine which assets require attention first.
4. **Plan:** Define migration options, ownership, testing, and milestones.
5. **Govern:** Monitor progress and maintain accountability.

## Reference Standards and Guidance

The framework draws on authoritative publications and guidance, including:

- [NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography Project](https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/post-quantum-cryptography)
- [NIST Cybersecurity Framework](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework)
- [NIST Computer Security Resource Center Publications](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications)

The toolkit will be updated as relevant standards and guidance evolve. Organizations should assess applicability to their own technology environments and regulatory obligations.

## Project Status

The toolkit is under active development. Initial deliverables will focus on cryptographic inventory, readiness assessment, risk prioritization, and migration planning.

## Scope and Limitations

This toolkit supports security assessment and migration planning. It is not a cryptographic implementation, certification, or assurance of security. Assessment results require validation against the organization's environment, applicable standards, and vendor guidance.
